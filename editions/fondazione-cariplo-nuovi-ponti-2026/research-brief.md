# Research brief — Fondazione Cariplo, "Nuovi ponti culturali"

- **Funder / host:** Fondazione Cariplo (Milan) — the major banking foundation of INLAND's home region.
- **Channel:** Public funding (private cultural foundation).
- **Link:** https://www.fondazionecariplo.it/bando/nuovi-ponti-culturali/
- **Deadline:** Rolling — **no deadline** ("senza scadenza"); proposals accepted at any time until the fund (€4,500,000 total) is exhausted.
- **Budget / award size:** Grant requested **€30,000–€150,000**, up to **70%** of total project cost.
- **Fit score:** **3 / 5** — possible, with a reframing + partner constraint.
- **One-line rationale:** The single most on-profile foundation in INLAND's own backyard and absent from the board, but it funds **non-profit cultural organisations** on an audience/representation theme, so INLAND accesses it as a publishing/documentation partner inside a Lombardy non-profit lead, not as sole applicant.

## Why it's relevant
Cariplo is named in `sources.md` (Channel 1 — "private cultural foundations … regional culture bandi, Lombardia"). "Nuovi ponti culturali" supports projects oriented either to **widening and diversifying audiences** ("Nuovi pubblici") or to **promoting artistic–cultural production that reflects the less-represented components of society** ("Pluralismo"). INLAND's method — long-form, dignified documentation of under-narrated living traditions — maps cleanly onto the *Pluralismo* strand if the territory is a genuinely under-represented community inside Lombardy/Piedmont.

## Eligibility (the binding constraint)
Applicants must be **private non-profit organisations** whose statute's principal purpose is running cultural activities or places, professionally active for **≥1 year**, based/active in **Lombardy or Piedmont**. They may apply singly, as a support network, or as lead of a partnership. **INLAND (an independent publisher) is almost certainly not an eligible lead applicant** — it joins as the documentation/publishing partner of a Lombardy/Piedmont non-profit cultural organisation (the Kashmir embassy-partner model, applied at home). *Confirm on the official bando whether a for-profit publisher may be a named partner and whether the €4.5M pot is still open.*

## Candidate territory / INLAND angle (within the eligible geography)
Set the territory by the funder's geography — **Lombardy/Piedmont** — and find the INLAND shape there (per `research-angle.md`, never default to Rwanda/Kashmir):
- **The Walser** — Germanic Alpine language islands of the Piedmont/VCO and upper-Lombardy valleys (Macugnaga, Alagna Valsesia, Formazza, Gressoney-adjacent): endangered Töitschu/Titsch speech, high-Alpine transhumance and the *alpeggio* summer, timber architecture and seasonal festivals. Named elder custodians, locatable villages, a real summer scene.
- **Orobie / Valtellina transhumance** — the *bergamini* herder families and mountain-dairy (formaggi d'alpe) cycle; the autumn *transumanza* down to the plain.
- **Cremona violin-making** (Lombardy, UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage) — named luthier workshops, a living master-to-apprentice craft; a strong "endangered knowledge held by named individuals" thread.

All three are under-represented in Italy's institutional cultural circuits, which answers the *Pluralismo* aim honestly.
