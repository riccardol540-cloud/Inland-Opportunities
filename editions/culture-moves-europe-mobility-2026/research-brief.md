# Research brief — Culture Moves Europe: Individual Mobility [RADAR: between rounds]

- **Title:** Culture Moves Europe — Call for Individual Mobility of Artists and Cultural Professionals
- **Funder / host:** European Union (Creative Europe), implemented by the **Goethe-Institut**
- **Channel:** cultural-institute / EU mobility
- **Link:** https://www.goethe.de/en/kul/foe/cmm.html · call listing: https://culture.ec.europa.eu
- **Deadline:** **RADAR — currently between rounds.** The 2025–26 call ran as a rolling monthly call and **closed 30 Apr 2026**. The date field (~2026-10-31) is an **ESTIMATE** for the next round, which historically re-opens in autumn with monthly review windows. Confirm on the Goethe/Creative Europe pages before treating as actionable.
- **Award:** Travel + subsistence contribution for a 7–60 day mobility (groups 7–21 days), with individual top-ups (green-travel, visa, family, special-needs). Not a production grant.

## Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies)
Open to **artists and cultural professionals legally resident in the 41 Creative Europe countries** (Italy included), 18+, active in an eligible sector (visual arts, literature, cultural heritage, etc.). Applications from **individuals or groups of up to five**. INLAND's editors/photographers qualify directly — no consortium, no co-financing.

## Fit — 3/5
The cleanest, lowest-burden EU route to **fund a scouting / field trip** for a future edition — exactly the "mobility = scouting" use already valued in the Goethe Africa–Europe entry on the board, but **intra-European and open to Italy**. Held at 3 because it covers **travel/subsistence only** (no production/print money) and is **between rounds** right now. Strategic value: it pays for the pre-production reconnaissance that unlocks a new territory, which the larger production funds then finance.

## Possible territory / angle
The grant funds travel **to another Creative Europe country**, so the destination should be a fresh, reachable INLAND territory. **Lead candidate: Portugal — Trás-os-Montes / Miranda do Douro.** The **Mirandese language** (Portugal's second official language, spoken by a shrinking community); the *pauliteiros* stick-dancers; and the *caretos* masked winter-solstice rituals (Podence — UNESCO ICH). Endangered language + masked ritual + locatable villages + a knowable season (winter) — a textbook INLAND lead set, and a country not yet on the board. Alternatives within eligible geography: Greece (Epirus polyphony & transhumance), Romania (Maramureș — already researched), or the northern-Albania Alps (ties to the Albania suggestion already on the board).

## Required application documents
- Description of the planned project and mobility (host contact in the destination country)
- Implementation plan and dates (7–60 days)
- Simple budget / justification for top-ups
- Applicant profile / portfolio
