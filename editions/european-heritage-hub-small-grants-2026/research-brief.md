# Research brief — European Heritage Hub Small Grants Scheme (2026)

**Funder:** European Heritage Hub (Europa Nostra-led consortium, co-funded by the European Union)
**Channel:** Partnership / co-production
**Link:** https://www.europeanheritagehub.eu/call-for-applications-small-grants-scheme-for-heritage-related-projects-led-by-civil-society-in-eu-candidate-and-neighbouring-countries/
**Deadline:** **7 September 2026 — URGENT** (call opened ~20 July 2026)
**Budget:** Small €2,000–10,000 · Medium €10,000–30,000 · Large €30,000–50,000
**Fit:** 3/5

## What it is

A grants scheme run by the European Heritage Hub (a Europa Nostra-led, EU-co-funded
initiative) supporting cultural-heritage projects **led by civil society in EU candidate
and neighbouring countries**. It funds projects that *preserve, promote and use cultural
heritage as a driver of sustainable development, democratic participation, peacebuilding
and social inclusion* — a frame that comfortably accommodates intangible-heritage
documentation (living traditions, oral culture, craft, ritual) of exactly the kind INLAND
produces.

Eligible geography spans three regional strands:
- **Western Balkans** — Albania, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia
- **South Caucasus** — Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia
- **Ukraine & Moldova**

Cross-border projects *between* eligible countries are explicitly encouraged.

## Why it matters to INLAND

This is the single genuinely on-profile, currently-open opportunity surfaced in this
week's scan, and its eligible map overlaps **three territories already live on the board**:

- **Albania** — the *pursued* edition (iso-polyphony custodians around
  Gjirokastër/Përmet; the northern-Alps Kanun world & the *burrnesha*; Aromanian/Vlach
  transhumance). A Western Balkans strand grant, led by an Albanian heritage NGO with
  INLAND as documentation/publishing partner, is a concrete funding route for it.
- **Georgia** and **Armenia** — both *research* editions (highland pastoral and women's
  lament traditions; Lake Sevan fishing cultures). The South Caucasus strand fits.
- The **cross-border Aromanian/Vlach angle** (Albania ↔ North Macedonia) is the sort of
  between-countries project the scheme says it prefers.

The register match is strong: intangible heritage, named custodians, locatable places,
seasonal scenes — INLAND's method, funded.

## The binding constraint (why it's a 3, not a 4)

**INLAND cannot be the lead applicant.** The scheme requires the lead to be a
**civil-society / not-for-profit organisation based in an eligible (non-EU) country**.
So this is actionable *only* as a partnership: an in-region heritage NGO (Albanian,
Georgian or Armenian) leads and implements; INLAND joins as the **documentation and
publishing partner** — the same embassy/partner-led model INLAND already uses for the
Kashmir pitch. Without a named in-region lead lined up before 7 September, this cycle is
not reachable and should be held for the next round.

## Required application documents (for the Producer, if pursued)

- Project concept: intangible cultural-heritage aims, named custodians/communities,
  locatable places, seasonal scenes
- **In-region civil-society / not-for-profit lead applicant** + INLAND as documentation/
  publishing partner (letters of intent)
- Budget by grant size (Small / Medium / Large) and work plan
- Timeline
- Partner track records (INLAND's published issues as proof of prior international
  cultural activity)

## To verify before committing

The official European Heritage Hub site blocked automated fetch during this scan.
Before acting, confirm on europeanheritagehub.eu:
- whether an **EU/Italy publisher may be a named (non-lead) partner** on an application;
- the **exact per-strand budgets**, eligible costs, and maximum project duration;
- the precise **7 Sep 2026** deadline and submission portal.

Distinct from the **CC4WBs** programme ("Culture and Creativity for the Western Balkans —
