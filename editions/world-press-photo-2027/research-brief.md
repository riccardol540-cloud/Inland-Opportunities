# Research brief — World Press Photo Contest 2027

- **Funder / host:** World Press Photo Foundation (Amsterdam)
- **Channel:** public fund (international award / prize)
- **Link:** https://www.worldpressphoto.org/contest
- **Deadline:** ~2027-01-15 (2027 cycle; **dates to confirm** — the contest opens ~1 Dec 2026 and closes mid-January on the fixed annual pattern; the 2026 edition ran 1 Dec 2025 → 17 Jan 2026).
- **Budget / award size:** Regional and global winners announced each spring; €1,000 per award, +€10,000 for the World Press Photo of the Year, plus a FUJIFILM camera for the year winner and two finalists. Global travelling exhibition + annual yearbook. **Free to enter.**
- **Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies):** Open worldwide to professional photojournalists and documentary photographers. INLAND applies via its commissioned edition photographer, entering an edition's documentary work. The contest accepts **singles**, **stories** (4–10 images) and **long-term projects** (24–30 images made over ≥3 years) inside a **regional** structure — the Stories and Long-Term Projects formats map directly onto an INLAND edition's photographic spine. Free entry = near-zero burden.
- **Fit score: 3/5.** Rationale: World Press Photo's Stories and Long-Term Projects categories regularly reward exactly the kind of place-based cultural-continuity documentary INLAND produces, and the platform (global exhibition tour, yearbook, press) is major visibility for a small independent publisher. Held at 3 because it is a prize/visibility play, not production funding, the applicant must be the individual photographer, and WPP's centre of gravity is news/current-affairs photojournalism — an INLAND submission must sit in the documentary/long-term strand, framed as continuity, not crisis.

## Possible territory / angle

**Open** — pairs with whichever edition holds the most complete, submission-ready documentary set, carried by its photographer. Grounded candidates: **Georgia** (Issue 02 highland custodians) or **Isole Minori** (Issue 03) as a finished **Stories** entry; or an in-progress edition's multi-year field work as a **Long-Term Project** (e.g. Tunisia water cultures; Rwanda/Angola). Enter under the regional Stories / Long-Term Projects strand, framed as endangered living traditions witnessed over a season — dignity over spectacle.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)

- A cohesive image set for the chosen format (Story 4–10 frames, or Long-Term Project 24–30 frames)
- Caption/context information for each frame (WPP requires accurate captions and verifiable context)
- Short project statement / narrative
- Photographer details / bio
- (No fee)
