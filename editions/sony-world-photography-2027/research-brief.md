# Research brief — Sony World Photography Awards 2027 (Series / Professional)

- **Funder / host:** Sony / World Photography Organisation
- **Channel:** public fund (international award / prize)
- **Link:** https://www.worldphoto.org/sony-world-photography-awards
- **Deadline:** 2027-01-12 (Series competition; 1 PM GMT). Open for entries now.
- **Budget / award size:** Photographer of the Year US$25,000 + Sony digital imaging equipment; category winners receive equipment; winning/shortlisted work exhibited at Somerset House (London) and published in the awards book. **Free to enter.**
- **Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies):** Open worldwide to photographers at all career stages, shooting on any device; the **Series** competition (formerly "Professional") rewards a cohesive body of 5–10 images. INLAND applies via its commissioned edition photographer, entering an edition's documentary series. Free entry = essentially zero-burden.
- **Fit score: 3/5.** Rationale: the Series competition's documentary strand is a natural home for an INLAND edition's photographic spine, and the platform (Somerset House exhibition, global press, awards book) is serious visibility for a small independent publisher. Held at 3 because it is a prize/visibility play, not production funding, and the applicant must be the individual photographer — but the free, low-effort entry makes it an easy opportunistic submission.

## Possible territory / angle

**Open** — pairs with whichever edition holds the most complete, exhibition-ready series, carried by its photographer. Lead candidates: **Georgia** (Issue 02 highland custodians) or **Isole Minori** (Issue 03) as a finished 10-frame documentary set; or an in-progress edition's field work (Tunisia water cultures; Rwanda/Angola). Enter under the documentary storytelling strand, framed as cultural continuity — not spectacle.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)

- A cohesive image series (typically up to 10 frames) for the chosen category
- Short project statement / series narrative
- Photographer details / bio
- (No fee)
