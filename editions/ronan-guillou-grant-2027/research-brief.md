# Research brief — Ronan Guillou Grant 2026/2027 (4th edition), "Eyes Wide Open"

- **Funder / host:** The Eyes Wide Open association (Ronan Guillou Grant), France — residency in Sète, exhibition at Musée de l'Étang de Thau (Bouzigues) as part of the FLOW Biennial.
- **Channel:** public-fund (private grant / photography award + residency)
- **Link:** https://theeyeswideopen.eu/en/the-ronan-guillou-grants/
- **Deadline:** **2026-11-15, 18:00** — ⚠️ **URGENT** (≤ 21 days from the 2026-11-11 scan; ~4 days out)
- **Budget / award:** €4,000 grant + a 2-month residency in Sète (Feb–Jun 2027) + a solo presentation in September 2027 at the Musée de l'Étang de Thau (Bouzigues), within the FLOW Biennial.
- **Eligibility:** Professional photographers from Europe and the southern shores of the Mediterranean. INLAND (Italy/EU) qualifies via a commissioned photographer.
- **Required documents:** Bio; project statement; portfolio. No entry fee.

## Fit — 3/5 (possible, with a tailored angle)

The subject — **humanity's future in synergy with the sea** — is squarely in INLAND's "human–nature relationship under ecological pressure" theme, and the house already proved the register with **Issue 03 (Isole Minori)**: fishing economies, island craft, lagoon and coastal custodians. A coastal/lagoon body of work built around a named fisher-family, a seasonal catch or a disappearing boat-building or salt-working practice would read as INLAND.

What holds it at 3 rather than higher:

1. **Register tension.** The call asks projects to "defend a progressive — or militant — vision to radically raise awareness." That advocacy framing runs against INLAND's discipline of *continuity over crisis, tension not attack, dignity over spectacle*. A fit requires reframing to INLAND's restraint while still answering the brief — doable but not automatic.
2. **Fixed French geography / residency burden.** The award is anchored to a **2-month on-site residency in Sète (Occitanie)** and an exhibition on the Étang de Thau. The 2025 edition required projects to reference the Occitanie region (thematic, geographic or symbolic); whether that constraint carries to this edition is **unconfirmed** — must be checked on the official call. This is a residency+exhibition opportunity, not open edition-territory funding.
3. **Modest budget** (€4k) — a production/visibility foothold, not edition-scale funding.

## Candidate territory / angle

**Étang de Thau / Sète lagoon (Occitanie, France)** if the geographic reference holds — oyster and mussel farmers of Bouzigues, the *jouteurs* and small-craft fishers of the Thau lagoon, salt and lagoon-edge livelihoods under warming-water and tourism pressure. Alternatively, if the geography is open, a **Mediterranean island/coastal** angle bridging to the Isole Minori register (e.g. lagoon/fishing custodians of the southern-shore Mediterranean).

## Required application documents (for the Producer)

- Project statement (sea/future synergy angle, reframed to INLAND's restrained voice)
- Photographer bio
- Portfolio / prior cohesive body of work (Issue 03 Isole Minori is the natural prior work)
- Note the applicant photographer, consents, and whether the 2-month Sète residency is feasible
