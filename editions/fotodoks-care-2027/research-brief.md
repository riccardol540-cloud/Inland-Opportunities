# Research brief — FOTODOKS Open Call 2027, theme "Care"

- **Funder / host:** FOTODOKS — Festival for Contemporary Documentary Photography, Munich (13th edition, October 2027).
- **Channel:** partnership (festival exhibition commission / open call)
- **Link:** https://fotodoks.de
- **Deadline:** **2026-11-20** — ⚠️ **URGENT** (≤ 21 days from the 2026-11-11 scan; ~9 days out). Results early spring 2027 (≈ March 2027).
- **Budget / award:** **Free to enter.** Selected photographers receive an **artist fee from €500** (collectives more), **with travel costs paid**, and are exhibited at the festival in Munich, October 2027.
- **Eligibility:** Open internationally; individuals, duos and collectives. Submission is **one existing project of 5–10 images** on the theme, in English or German.
- **Theme:** **"Care"** — existing lens-based documentary stories responding to care.

## Fit — 3/5 (possible; low-burden visibility foothold)

FOTODOKS is a serious **contemporary documentary photography** festival — INLAND's exact register — and the theme **"Care"** maps cleanly onto INLAND's spine: custodianship and the *care of* endangered knowledge, land, ritual and kin (the care a shepherd gives a herd, a cook gives a grain, a singer gives an oral form). Crucially, the call asks for **existing** work, so INLAND can submit a finished body (Faroe / Georgia / Isole Minori) rather than producing anything new — a near-zero-burden entry.

What holds it at 3: it is an **exhibition/visibility** opportunity with a modest artist fee (from €500 + travel), **not edition funding**. Value is reach, curatorial validation, and a German documentary-scene relationship — a foothold, not a territory unlock. The board already carries several festival open calls of this shape.

## Candidate territory / angle

No new territory required — this is best used to **place an existing INLAND body of work** under the "Care" frame. Strongest candidates: Isole Minori (island custodianship and care of place), or a Georgia / Faroe sequence foregrounding care of tradition and kin. If a fresh angle is wanted: a care-centred edit on pastoral or food custodians from any in-progress edition.

## Required application materials (for the Producer)

- One project of **5–10 images** on "Care" (an edit from an existing INLAND edition)
- Short project statement (English or German) tying the work to care
- Photographer / contributor credits
- (No fee)
