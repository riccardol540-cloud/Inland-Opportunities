# Research brief — Journalismfund Europe: European Cross-border Grants Programme

- **Funder / host:** Journalismfund Europe (Brussels)
- **Channel:** public fund (cross-border journalism) — doubles as a partnership route
- **Link:** https://www.journalismfund.eu/grants (application platform: https://grants.journalismfund.eu)
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-01 (next round; 1 PM CET). Four rounds a year — 2026 deadlines: 19 Mar, 21 May, 30 Jul, **1 Oct**. Near-deadline but recurring, so a missed round rolls to the next.
- **Budget / award size:** €280,000 available per round; grants cover research-related costs — reporting, travel, legal checks, expert support. Paid in two instalments (2/3 on signature, 1/3 after publication).
- **Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies):** Open to journalists (staff or freelance) working in a cross-border **team spanning at least two European countries**. INLAND applies via its commissioned photographer/writer joined with a reporter/collaborator in a second European country. Crucially, **all journalistic end products qualify — explicitly including photo-reportages and non-fiction books** — which is INLAND's exact output.
- **Fit score: 3/5.** Rationale: a genuine production/reporting-cost route (not a prize) whose cross-border framing is a natural home for an INLAND borderland/minority edition, and whose eligible formats name photo-reportage and books outright. Held at 3 by the journalism register (leans reporting/"societal impact" over slow anthropological documentation) and the two-country-team requirement — INLAND must line up a collaborator in a second country.

## Possible territory / angle

**Lead candidate — the Aromanian / Vlach world of the southern Balkans** (a cross-border theme already flagged in the Creative Europe Cooperation entry): a stateless, Latin-language pastoral people whose transhumance, polyphonic song and craft survive across **Greece, Albania, North Macedonia and Romania**. A two-country cross-border team (e.g. an Italian INLAND lead + a Greek or Albanian reporter/photographer) can follow one named shepherd-cantor lineage and the **summer move to high pasture** as the seasonal spine — a locatable, photographable, genuinely cross-border story that satisfies the programme's core requirement by construction.

Alternative: the **Arbëreshë (Italy) ↔ Albania** dialogue (endangered language, ritual laments, Easter *Vallja*), pairing an Italian and an Albanian collaborator.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)

- Cross-border project proposal (angle, the ≥2 countries involved, news value / relevance)
- Confirmed cross-border team (collaborator in a second European country) + roles
- Budget (research, travel, reporting, expert/legal costs)
- Distribution / publication plan (the INLAND issue + any partner outlet)
- Journalist(s) CV / portfolio
