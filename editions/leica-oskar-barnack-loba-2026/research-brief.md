# Leica Oskar Barnack Award (LOBA) — research brief

*Scout scan 2026-09-02. RADAR entry: the 2026 cycle has been judged (winners announced
8 October 2026); this is an annual award, logged now so INLAND can plan the applicable
route into the next (2027) cycle.*

- **Title:** Leica Oskar Barnack Award (LOBA) — humanist documentary photography (humanity & the environment)
- **Funder / host:** Leica Camera AG
- **Channel:** Public fund / award (cash prize + equipment; touring visibility)
- **Link:** https://www.leica-oskar-barnack-award.com
- **Deadline:** 2026 cycle judged (winners announced **8 Oct 2026**). Annual. Next cycle 2027 — **confirm the submission window on the official site** (the open-submission Women Grant historically opens in the first half of the year).
- **Budget / award:** **Main Award €40,000** + €10,000 Leica equipment; **Newcomer Award €10,000** (photographers under 30) + a Leica Q3; ten other finalists €2,500 each. A separate **LOBA Women Grant** supports a professional woman photographer's project.
- **Eligibility:** Professional photographers, international.
  - **Main & Newcomer Awards are nomination-only** — a network of 130+ nominators (photography experts in ~48 countries) each propose photo series of 15–20 images; you cannot apply directly.
  - **LOBA Women Grant is open to free submission** by professional female photographers aged 21+, with a meaningful project outline — the directly-applicable route for INLAND.

## Fit — 3/5

Thematically a bullseye and a prestige prize, held to a 3 by the access mechanics. LOBA's
standing subject is exactly one of INLAND's core themes: **works of documentary or
conceptual photography that explore the relationship between humanity and the
environment** — i.e. human–nature relationships under ecological/climate pressure
(research-angle.md's pastoralism / transhumance / agrarian-life-as-climate-rewrites-it
category). The register (restrained, humanist, long-form) is INLAND's own.

Two routes, both real but neither a simple open application:

1. **LOBA Women Grant (open):** INLAND applies via a **commissioned woman photographer**
   with a project outline on a human–environment thread. This overlaps with the
   PhMuseum Women grant already on the board but is a larger, more prestigious platform.
2. **Main/Newcomer Award (nomination-only):** not directly applicable — the strategic
   action is to **get an INLAND edition's photographer onto a LOBA nominator's radar**
   (curators, critics, festival directors in the nominator network). Worth cultivating
   over the year, not a same-cycle deliverable.

## Territory / angle

**Open** — pairs with any in-progress INLAND edition, carried by its photographer, on a
**human–nature** thread. Strong matches in existing research: **Kashmir** Gujjar–Bakkarwal
migration / apple growers and hailstorms; **Romania**/**Georgia** transhumance; **Tunisia**
island and oasis water cultures — each a knowable-season, human-and-environment story.

## Required application documents (LOBA Women Grant route, for the Producer)

- Project outline / statement on the human–environment theme (a woman photographer's project)
- Image edit / series (documentary or conceptual)
- Photographer bio / professional track record (professional, aged 21+)
