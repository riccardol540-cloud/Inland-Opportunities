# Research brief — CEI Cooperation Fund [RADAR: 2026 closed, annual]

- **Title:** CEI Cooperation Fund — Call for Proposals (cultural cooperation among CEI member states)
- **Funder / host:** Central European Initiative (CEI), Trieste — financed by member-state contributions
- **Channel:** partnership (cross-border cultural cooperation)
- **Link:** https://www.cei.int/ (Cooperation Activities / Cooperation Fund)
- **Deadline:** **RADAR — the 2026 call CLOSED 29 May 2026, 12:00 CEST.** The CEI Cooperation Fund is an **annual** call; the date field (~2027-05-29) is an **ESTIMATE** for the next cycle — confirm on cei.int when the 2027 call publishes. **Not currently open.**
- **Award:** Co-financing for cooperation actions (typically up to ~€25,000 per project, up to ~50% of costs — confirm current ceiling and rate on the 2027 call), funding **innovative actions favouring cultural cooperation, mobility and networking** among CEI member states.

## Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies)
Open to **public and private institutions registered in a CEI Member State, NGOs/CSOs and international organisations.** **Italy is a founding CEI member**, so — unlike the European Heritage Hub small grants, where INLAND cannot be the lead applicant — **INLAND can be a lead or co-applicant here.** Crucially, the CEI membership spans exactly INLAND's Balkan/Eastern target geography: **Albania, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czechia, Hungary, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Romania, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Ukraine** and others, alongside Italy. A project needs partners from **at least two–three CEI member states**.

## Fit — 3/5
A genuine **partnership-channel** route (the third agreed channel) where **INLAND can lead**, in a geography that already carries three board territories (**Albania** pursued; **Romania** pursued; and the wider Balkans). The cross-border cooperation/mobility framing is a natural home for an INLAND **borderland / minority / cultural-continuity** edition. Held at **3** by the **modest grant size** (a seed/cooperation contribution, not edition-production funding), the **~50% co-financing** expectation, and the multi-country-partner requirement — best used as a **relationship + mobility foothold** that seeds a larger production bid (e.g. Creative Europe Cooperation).

## Possible territory / angle
A theme spanning several CEI member states satisfies both the partner requirement and the INLAND angle:
- **Italy–Albania (Arbëreshë ↔ Albania)** — the Albanian-speaking Byzantine-rite communities of southern Italy in dialogue with Albania: endangered language, ritual laments, *Vallja* dances (pairs with the pursued **Albania** edition, `opp-1782981837927`).
- **The Aromanian / Vlach world** — a stateless Latin-language pastoral people whose transhumance, polyphonic song and craft survive across **Albania, North Macedonia, Romania** (all CEI members), with an Italian lead partner — borderlands + minority language + endangered knowledge in one frame.
- **Danube / Maramureș threads** tied to the pursued **Romania** edition (`opp-1782290582161`).

## Required application documents
- Project description (cooperation objectives, cross-border/mobility dimension, cultural value)
- Partners from ≥2–3 CEI member states + letters of support
- Budget showing the required co-financing (~50%) and activity plan
- Timeline / work plan; applicant & partner profiles (INLAND's published issues as track record)
