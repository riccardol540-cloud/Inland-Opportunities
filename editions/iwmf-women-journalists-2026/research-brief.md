# Research brief — IWMF Fund for Women Journalists (Howard G. Buffett Fund)

- **Title:** Fund for Women Journalists (Howard G. Buffett Fund for Women Journalists)
- **Funder / host:** International Women's Media Foundation (IWMF)
- **Channel:** public-fund (documentary / journalism grant)
- **Link:** https://www.iwmf.org/programs/fund-for-women-journalists/ · rolling submission portal via iwmf.submittable.com
- **Deadline:** **Rolling** — no fixed cut-off; applications reviewed continuously, decisions ~4–6 weeks after submission. (The date field carries a nominal placeholder, not a real deadline.)
- **Award:** Grants for reporting projects — typically a few thousand USD toward reporting costs, travel and production (confirm the current ceiling on the portal; historically up to ~US$2,000 for the Buffett rolling stream, larger for special calls).

## Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies)
Open **worldwide** to **women and non-binary journalists**, staff or **freelance**, across all media platforms — **print, photography, audio, video**. INLAND applies **via a commissioned woman journalist or photographer** who carries an edition's field body of work. The grant funds the *reporting*, so the applicant is the individual storyteller, not the publisher — the same structure INLAND already uses for the Pulitzer, Smith and PhMuseum routes on the board.

## Fit — 3/5
A genuine, **currently-open, rolling** route with worldwide eligibility and a documentary register that fits INLAND. Held at 3 because it is a **journalism reporting grant** (modest budget, applicant must be the woman journalist, and it rewards a defined *story* rather than the full field year). Its distinct value versus the Pulitzer entry already on the board: it is **women-and-non-binary-specific** and **always open**, so it can be timed to a commissioned woman photographer/writer whenever an edition's women-led thread is ready.

## Possible territory / angle
Because the fund backs under-reported **women's stories**, pair it with a women-carried, women-subject INLAND thread. **Lead candidate: Sardinia — Barbagia (Italy).** The *attittu* — ritual mourning laments sung by women (the *attittadoras*, near-vanished professional mourners) — plus women's ceremonial bread-making (Su Coccoi, Pane Carasau festival breads). Named women custodians, a locatable interior (Orgosolo, Orune, Oliena), and seasonal scenes (funerary rites; feast-day bread). Under-documented internationally, women at the centre — a natural IWMF frame that also seeds an Italy-anchored edition. Alternative women-led threads already researched: Georgian polyphonic lament, Rwandan/Angolan *ibyivugo* women's oral poetry, Tunisian craft women.

## Required application documents
- Reporting proposal (topic, location, angle, why under-reported)
- Budget (travel and production)
- Journalist/photographer CV + work samples / portfolio
- (For some streams) a distribution/publication plan or outlet indication
