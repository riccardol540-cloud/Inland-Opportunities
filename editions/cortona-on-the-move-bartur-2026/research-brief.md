- **Title:** Cortona On The Move / BarTur Grant — documentary photography production grant
- **Funder / host:** Cortona On The Move (Italian international photography festival) with the BarTur Photo Award
- **Channel:** Public fund / grant (production grant + festival exhibition)
- **Link:** https://www.cortonaonthemove.com/en/grant/ · https://www.barturphotoaward.org
- **Deadline:** 2026 cycle **closed 16 April 2026**. Annual. Next cycle expected ≈ March–April 2027 — **confirm exact date on the official site.**
- **Budget / award:** **US$30,000 grand prize** (one professional photographer) + **two US$5,000 prizes** for emerging photographers under 30. Winners get ~9 months to complete the project; finished work is exhibited at Cortona On The Move (edition following the award, e.g. July 2027).
- **Eligibility:** Open **internationally**. The grand prize is open to professional photographers (no age cap); the two smaller prizes are reserved for emerging photographers under 30. INLAND applies **via a commissioned edition photographer** presenting an in-progress documentary body of work.

## Fit — 4/5

Strong, broadly-applicable documentary fit. The grant funds the **completion of an
in-progress documentary project addressing urgent social, environmental, economic and
cultural issues** — exactly the register of an INLAND edition's field body of work — and
rewards it with a cash production grant plus a show at one of Europe's most respected
documentary-photography festivals, in **Italy** (INLAND's base). It sits naturally
alongside the board's other photographer-applicant grants (W. Eugene Smith, Carmignac,
Tom Stoddart), but with an Italian-festival exhibition foothold that the others lack.

Held just below a 5 because the applicant must be the photographer (not INLAND directly),
and the "urgent issues" framing needs INLAND's *continuity-over-crisis* discipline
(research-angle.md): pitch the cultural-continuity and human–nature threads (a living
tradition under pressure), not a crisis exposé.

## Territory / angle

**Open** — pairs with any in-progress INLAND edition, carried by its commissioned
photographer. Best-matched threads from existing research: the **Georgia** highland
custodians (W. Eugene Smith brief), **Romania** transhumance shepherds (ICR/CANTEMIR
brief), or the **Rwanda/Angola** and **Tunisia** field work — any of which supplies a
cohesive, in-progress documentary series on cultural continuity under ecological or
economic pressure.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)

- Project proposal on an in-progress documentary body of work (cultural/social/environmental angle)
- Image edit of work already underway
- Photographer bio / CV and professional track record
- Production plan / how the grant completes the project (~9-month timeline to exhibition)
