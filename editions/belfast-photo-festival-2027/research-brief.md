# Research brief — Belfast Photo Festival 2027 International Open Call

**Channel:** Partnership / festival (visibility + exhibition)
**Funder / host:** Belfast Photo Festival (Belfast, Northern Ireland / UK)
**Link:** https://www.belfastphotofestival.com/
**Deadline:** **5 February 2027** (early-bird fee until 11 December 2026)
**Fit score:** 3/5

## What it is
The Belfast Photo Festival Open Submission — an international juried open call for
inclusion in the festival's public exhibition programme (festival runs **3–27 June
2027**). There is **no set theme**: the organisers seek original, distinctive bodies of
work on any subject. The draw is the **jury calibre** — jurors are drawn from **Tate,
MoMA, Centre Pompidou, PhotoVogue, The New York Times, the Independent Photography
Festival Association (IPFA)** and the festival itself — so even selection (not only a
win) is a meaningful credential and introduction.

- **Entry fee:** £22 per entry early-bird (until 23:59 GMT, 11 December 2026), then £26
  standard to the 5 February 2027 deadline.
- **Eligibility:** open worldwide to photographers, artists, curators, archives and
  collectives aged 16+. Photographic / lens-based work only.
- **Results:** late March / early April 2027.

## Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies)
Fully open internationally; an INLAND photographer or the collective can enter a
documentary series. No nationality or institutional barrier.

## Fit rationale
Like Fotofestiwal, this is a **visibility foothold, not production money** — there is no
cash grant, only exhibition and jury exposure. Held at 3. What lifts it above a routine
festival call is the **no-theme freedom** (an INLAND edition's existing essay fits with
zero reframing) and a jury that reaches the exact institutions — Pompidou, Tate, MoMA,
PhotoVogue — INLAND wants relationships with for future partnerships and co-productions.

## Possible territory / angle
Open. Submit a finished, people-anchored series from a current edition. The festival's
large, discovery-oriented open programme suits INLAND's slow, custodian-led frames;
lead with one named protagonist and a single locatable place per image cluster.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)
- A body of work (image series) via the festival's Picter submission
- Project title + statement
- Entry fee (£22 early-bird if submitted by 11 Dec 2026, else £26)
