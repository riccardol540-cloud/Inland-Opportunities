# Research brief — IDFA Bertha Fund (IBF Europe)

- **Title:** IDFA Bertha Fund — IBF Europe (minority co-production scheme for creative documentary)
- **Funder / host:** IDFA Bertha Fund, Amsterdam (the IDFA documentary festival's fund)
- **Channel:** Partnership / co-production
- **Link:** https://professionals.idfa.nl/training-funding/funding/about-the-idfa-bertha-fund/
- **Deadline:** 2027-01-15 (IBF Europe; entries open 15 Nov 2026). *Not urgent.* (Sister scheme IBF Classic: 10 Feb 2027.)
- **Budget / award:** Up to **€40,000** per project (co-production contribution).
- **Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies):** The applicant must be the **European minority co-producer** (holding ≥20% of the co-production) from a Creative Europe **MEDIA** country — **INLAND (Italy) qualifies** — on a **creative documentary of ≥60 min** whose **director is from an IBF-eligible country** (Global-South / countries not yet fully integrated into international co-production). Documentary series and immersive/interactive projects are **not** eligible; projects must be mid-production to post-production.

## Fit — 3 (adjacent but genuinely usable)
This funds the **film strand** of an INLAND edition, not the print magazine — so it is a complement to, not a replacement for, the publication funds already on the board. It is usable because INLAND already commissions films alongside each issue, and the IBF Europe structure (one European + one non-European producer, European holding the minority share) **maps directly onto the INLAND edition model**: a non-EU territory, a local lead, INLAND as the European partner. The constraint is real: INLAND cannot lead — it must be the ≤-minority co-producer behind an eligible-country director.

## Candidate territory / angle (per research-angle.md)
Territory is set by the eligible-country partner, so the natural fit is INLAND's **non-EU in-progress edition**:

- **Rwanda** (in-progress) — a **Rwandan-led creative documentary** on a single living-tradition custodian (e.g. the *inanga* zither's possible last master, or foot-crushed banana-beer *urwagwa* as social infrastructure), with INLAND as the European minority co-producer. Rwanda sits on the IBF-eligible side; Italy is the MEDIA-country minority partner.
- *Note:* Georgia is now a Creative Europe country, so a Georgian director likely reads as "European" and would **not** satisfy the non-EU-lead requirement — keep Rwanda (or another IBF-list territory) as the lead.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)
- Co-production agreement (European minority ≥20% + non-EU lead producer)
- Director from an IBF-eligible country (verify against the IBF Europe country list)
- Creative documentary ≥60 min, mid-/post-production stage
- Budget & financing plan
- Project description / treatment

## Open questions / field-verification
- Confirm the current **IBF Europe country list** and the 20% minority-share rule with idfaberthafund@idfa.nl before committing.
- Requires a **lead non-EU producer/director** partner — the single biggest dependency; pair this with whichever edition has (or can secure) a local filmmaker.
- Confirm the film is at mid-/post-production stage by the deadline (development-stage films are out of scope here).
