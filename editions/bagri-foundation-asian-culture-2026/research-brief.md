# Research brief — Bagri Foundation, Arts & Culture Grants

- **Funder / host:** The Bagri Foundation (UK).
- **Channel:** Partnership / cultural institute (grant-making foundation for Asian culture).
- **Link:** https://www.bagrifoundation.org/ (Arts & Culture Grants programme)
- **Deadline:** Annual, two-stage. The 2026/27 round's Expression of Interest ran **9 Feb – 11 Mar 2026** (full applications 17 Apr 2026) — **closed**; the next round is expected to **re-open in autumn 2026**. RADAR — confirm the next EOI window.
- **Budget / award size:** **£5,000–£150,000** (typically 10–40% of total project cost; contact the Foundation before requesting >£75,000).
- **Fit score:** **3 / 5** — adjacent, with a double eligibility constraint.
- **One-line rationale:** A rare grant that could underwrite INLAND's **Asian research editions** (Uzbekistan, Kashmir), but it funds **UK not-for-profit hosts** and requires **artists from Asia or its diasporas**, so INLAND reaches it only through a UK charity partner + an Asian-diaspora collaborator — a structured partnership, not a direct application.

## Why it's relevant
INLAND carries two active Asian territories — **Uzbekistan** (research edition) and **Kashmir** (in-progress) — that currently have little dedicated funding on the board. Bagri funds projects that "promote the sharing of traditional or contemporary Asian culture," exactly INLAND's register when applied to an Asian edition, at a meaningful production scale (up to £150k). It also complements the British Council CTC entry already on the board (both are UK-anchored partnership routes), pointing to a repeatable "UK not-for-profit host" pattern for INLAND.

## Eligibility (the binding constraints)
- Applicant must be a **UK registered charity, CIO, CIC, exempt charity, or experienced creative collective** with a track record of delivering artistic projects — **individuals are not eligible**, and a for-profit Italian publisher is **not** an eligible lead.
- The project must **include established or mid-career artists from Asia or the diasporas** and result in a **tangible public outcome** (exhibition, publication, programme).
- Grant covers only **10–40%** of project cost → co-financing required.

So INLAND participates as the editorial/publishing partner inside a project **led by a UK not-for-profit**, foregrounding an **Asian or Asian-diaspora artist/custodian** as a principal — e.g. a Kashmiri or Uzbek photographer/craftsperson.

## Candidate territory / INLAND angle
Set by INLAND's existing Asian pipeline (per `research-angle.md`, found afresh — the loud story kept as background):
- **Uzbekistan** — Boysun spring rituals (UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage), Bukhara *suzani* embroidery and its last master artisans, Karakalpak life on the dried Aral. Named custodians, locatable places, seasonal scenes.
- **Kashmir** (in-progress) — carried by a Kashmiri-diaspora artist as a principal (Baand Pather theatre, *wanwun* wedding songs, saffron on the Karewas), with the UK host providing a public outcome (exhibition + publication).

## Required application documents (so the Producer knows what to build)
- Expression of Interest, then full application, submitted by the **UK not-for-profit lead**
- Project concept promoting the sharing of Asian culture, naming the **Asia/diaspora artist(s)** and the tangible public outcome
- Budget showing co-financing (grant = 10–40% of costs)
- Timeline / delivery plan
- Track records (UK host; INLAND's published issues; the Asian artist's practice)
