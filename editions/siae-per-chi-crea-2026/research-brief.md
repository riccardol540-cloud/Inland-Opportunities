# Research brief — SIAE "Per Chi Crea" 2026 (MiC × SIAE, under-35 creation fund)

- **Funder / host:** SIAE (Società Italiana degli Autori ed Editori) on behalf of the Ministero della Cultura (MiC) — the "Per Chi Crea" programme, financed from the SIAE *copia privata* levy.
- **Channel:** public fund (Italian national culture fund).
- **Link:** https://www.perchicrea.it (bandi + portal) · programme page: https://www.siae.it/it/perchicrea/
- **Deadline:** **2026-10-29, 14:00 (Italian time)** — single submission deadline for the 2026 edition; winners published by 1 March 2027. Not urgent (≈6 weeks out at scan date), but a hard, non-recurring-within-year deadline.
- **Budget / award size:** Over **€13 million** across the edition's bandi (the largest Italian public instrument aimed squarely at young creators). Per-project grants vary by bando/strand (historically in the low tens of thousands for creation strands, covering production of new work).
- **Eligibility (why INLAND could qualify):** Aimed at **young authors, artists and performers under 35**, resident in Italy or Italian citizens resident abroad — applying as individuals **or as groups/collectives** of under-35s (and, in some strands, non-profit entities whose creative team is under-35). Sectors covered include **arti visive (incl. photography), letteratura/editoria, multimedia**, plus performing arts, cinema, dance, music, theatre — i.e. INLAND's photographic + editorial output is in scope. INLAND applies by structuring the project around its under-35 commissioned authors (photographer/writer) or a young-creator collective, on a new Italian body of work.
- **Fit score: 3/5.** Rationale: a large, well-funded Italian public fund that explicitly covers photography and publishing and would anchor a *domestic* INLAND edition — but held at 3 by the **under-35 eligibility constraint**, which forces the application to be built around young authors rather than the house/founder, and by the creation-strand framing (production of a new work, not institutional edition funding). A clean structural fit *if* the young-author framing is workable.

## Possible territory / angle

**Italy — the under-documented interior**, told INLAND-style. Issue 03 (*Isole Minori*) already proves the domestic register; this fund pushes toward the mainland's quiet country. Candidate threads (each needs a named custodian, a locatable place, a seasonal scene):

- **Appennino transhumance & pastoral craft** — the surviving droving routes and shepherd-cheesemakers of the central/southern Apennines (Abruzzo–Molise *tratturi*), a seasonal move that still happens on a knowable calendar.
- **Language islands** — the **Arbëreshë** (Albanian-speaking, Byzantine-rite Calabria/Basilicata/Sicily) or **Griko** (Salento) communities: endangered tongue, ritual laments, Easter *Vallja* — an Italy-anchored angle that also bridges to the board's Albania/Allianz Arbëreshë routes.
- **Sardinian oral polyphony** — *cantu a tenore* lineages and pastoral ritual in the Barbagia.

The territory is Italian, so no cross-border partner is required — an advantage over the board's EU-consortium routes.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)

- Project description of the new work (concept, custodians/places/seasonal scenes, Italian cultural value)
- Proof of eligibility — under-35 author(s)/collective, Italian residence or Italian citizenship abroad
- Budget (production of the new work) and timeline
- Team CVs / portfolio of the young author(s)
- Any partner/host indication for the field and publication phases

## Notes / caveats

- **Verify on the primary source before applying.** The `perchicrea.it` bando pages were egress-blocked from the scan environment; the deadline (29 Oct 2026), the >€13M envelope, the under-35 rule and the sector list are confirmed from SIAE's own notice and reputable secondaries, but the **exact strand** (e.g. *Nuove Opere* for creation) and its per-project ceiling, the individual-vs-collective-vs-entity rules, and any co-financing terms must be read off the specific 2026 bando before the Producer builds the application.
- **Eligibility is the decisive question:** confirm INLAND can front the application through its under-35 authors or a young-creator collective. If the house cannot be structured under the age rule for any strand, drop to fit 2 / archive.
- Register discipline unchanged: continuity over crisis, dignity over spectacle, named custodians — the *Isole Minori* method applied to a new Italian interior.
