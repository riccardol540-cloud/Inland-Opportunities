# Research brief — Italian Council (DGCC / MiC) [RADAR: Ed. 15 closed Mar 2026, annual]

- **Title:** Italian Council — programme to promote Italian visual art and international editorial projects abroad (Ed. 15 ref.)
- **Funder / host:** Ministero della Cultura — Direzione Generale Creatività Contemporanea (DGCC)
- **Channel:** public-fund (Italian national fund)
- **Link:** https://creativitacontemporanea.cultura.gov.it/italiancouncil-edizione15/ (DGCC Bandi Portal)
- **Deadline:** **RADAR — the 15th edition (2026) CLOSED 31 Mar 2026** (portal opened 20 Feb 2026). Italian Council is an **annual** DGCC call; DGCC typically opens it in **late February for a late-March/April deadline**. The date field (~2027-03-31) is an **ESTIMATE** for the next (16th) edition — confirm on the DGCC Call Portal when the 2027 avviso publishes. **Not currently open.**
- **Award:** ~**€2.7M** total (Ed. 15). Area 1–2 grants fund exhibitions, biennials, curatorial commissions abroad and **international publishing / editorial projects**; Area 3 offers **€15,000 research/residency grants** to Italian (or Italy-tax-resident) artists, **photographers**, curators, critics and art professionals for a ~15-month project abroad.

## Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies)
The programme supports the **internationalisation of Italian visual art and creativity**, so the project must promote **Italian** artistic/photographic/editorial work abroad. INLAND (Italy-based publisher, Italian photographers/editors) qualifies through **Area 1–2 international editorial projects** — an Italy-anchored edition produced by Italian authors and distributed/launched internationally — and its photographers/editors qualify for **Area 3** research grants. This is arguably the **single most on-profile Italian national fund for INLAND's editorial output** and, until now, was **absent from the board** — it was explicitly named as a "sister call to watch" in the Strategia Fotografia notes.

## Fit — 4/5
€2.7M national fund whose Area 1–2 **"particular attention to international editorial projects"** maps almost exactly onto funding an INLAND issue for international audiences, and whose Area 3 grants can pay an edition's field photographer. Held at **4 (not 5)** by the framing constraint — the funded work must foreground **Italian** creativity abroad, so it fits an **Italy-anchored** edition (or the Italian-authored spine of any edition) rather than a purely foreign-territory issue — and by the fact it is currently between cycles.

## Possible territory / angle
Italy-anchored, Italian-authored, internationally distributed. Lead candidates from the INLAND angle:
- **The Arbëreshë** (Albanian-speaking, Byzantine-rite communities of Calabria/Basilicata/Sicily) — endangered language, ritual laments, Easter *Vallja* dances; a European/Mediterranean editorial project promoting an Italian minority culture abroad.
- **Sardinia** — pastoral & lagoon cultures (Barbagia *attittu* laments and ceremonial bread; Cabras reed-boat *fassoni* fishers), building on Issue 03 (*Isole Minori*).
- **Appennine "aree interne"** — depopulating interior Italy, transhumance and craft.
Each has named custodians, locatable places and seasonal scenes, carried by Italian photographers — a natural Area 1–2 international editorial project.

## Required application documents
- Project proposal per Area (Area 1–2 international editorial/publishing project; or Area 3 research/residency)
- Budget and work plan; for editorial projects, an international distribution/launch dimension
- Applicant credentials / track record (INLAND's published issues) and Italian authorship
- SPID/CIE registration on the DGCC Call Portal
