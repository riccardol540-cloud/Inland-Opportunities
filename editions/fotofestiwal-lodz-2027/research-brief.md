# Research brief — Fotofestiwal (Łódź) Open Call 2027

**Channel:** Partnership / festival (visibility + exhibition)
**Funder / host:** Fotofestiwal — International Festival of Photography, Łódź (organised by the Foundation of Visual Education)
**Link:** https://fotofestiwal.com/2026/en/open-program-2027/
**Deadline:** **30 November 2026** — **URGENT** (≈12 days from this scan)
**Fit score:** 3/5

## What it is
Open call for the Open Program of the 26th Fotofestiwal (Łódź, Poland), one of Central
Europe's longest-running international photography festivals. Up to **6 projects** are
selected for exhibition during the festival (June 2027). Each selected artist receives an
**artistic fee of €1,000**, plus accommodation during the opening weekend and
(per the organiser) up to ~€600 toward exhibition production and ~€300 travel
reimbursement. Selected artists may also be **nominated to join FUTURES**, the
EU-backed pan-European photography platform — the real strategic value here.

- **Entry fee:** €15 per project (max two projects per artist). Fee **waivers available**
  on request to opencall@fotofestiwal.com.
- **Submission:** via the Picter platform.
- **Results:** ~25 January 2027. **Exhibition:** June 2027 in Łódź.

## Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies)
Open to **artists and collectives from anywhere**, with **no restriction on subject
matter or age**. An INLAND-commissioned photographer, or INLAND as a collective, can
submit a coherent documentary body of work from a current or past edition. Lens-based /
documentary work is squarely in scope.

## Fit rationale
This is **not edition funding** — the cash is a modest artist fee, and the burden is low.
Its worth to INLAND is **visibility and relationships**: a respected international festival
slot plus a possible **FUTURES** nomination, which plugs a photographer into a
Europe-wide network of institutions and future calls. Same register as the Cortona and
FOTODOKS footholds already on the board. Held at 3 because it funds exposure, not a
field embed or a print run.

## Possible territory / angle
Open. Strongest submissions would be an existing INLAND body of work with named
custodians and seasonal scenes already shot — e.g. **Isole Minori (Issue 03)**, or the
**Georgia / Faroe** archives — presented as a tight 15–25 image series. No new territory
is required to apply; the call rewards a finished, locatable, people-first essay.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)
- A project (image series, 15–25 frames) uploaded via Picter
- Short project statement / description
- Artist or collective bio
- Entry fee €15 (or a waiver request email)

## Open questions / field-verification
- Confirm exact production/travel figures and max image count on the organiser's Picter
