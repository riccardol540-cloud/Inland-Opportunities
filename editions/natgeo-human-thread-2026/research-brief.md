- **Title:** The Human Thread (storytelling RFP on the origins and living expressions of cultures & religions)
- **Funder / host:** National Geographic Society — Human Histories & Cultures focus area (supported by Lilly Endowment Inc.)
- **Channel:** Public fund (society grant / RFP)
- **Link:** https://www.nationalgeographic.org/explore/grants-investments
- **Deadline:** 2026-12-01 (RFP window 1 Oct – 1 Dec 2026). *Not urgent (34 days out).*
- **Budget / award:** Level I up to **US$20,000** (applicants new to NGS funding, ~1 year); Level II up to **US$100,000** (established applicants, ~2 years).
- **Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies):** Open worldwide, applicants 18+, apply in English, submit a portfolio of prior media work. INLAND applies via/with its commissioned photographers and filmmakers; the Society prioritises applicants with **lived experience or established relationships** in the communities documented.

## Candidate territory / angle (per research-angle.md)
The RFP is open worldwide, so the territory is ours to set within INLAND's pipeline. Because the brief is framed around **cultures *and religions*, their origins and survivals**, the strongest leads are places where faith and ritual carry the deep memory of a people:

- **Armenia** (listed research edition) — one of the world's oldest Christian cultures living alongside vivid **pre-Christian survivals**: the midsummer water festival *Vardavar* (Astghik's rite), the *duduk* and sacred *sharakan* chant, highland pilgrimage, and the **Yazidi** shrine-keeping communities of the Aragatsotn plain. Named custodians, locatable monasteries/villages, and seasonal scenes are all findable.
- **Georgia** (Issue 02 territory) alternative — the **highland shrine faith** of Khevsureti and the *khati* shrine-keepers, Svan tower-village ritual — Orthodox form over older mountain belief.

Both honour the discipline: the loud national story stays background; the lens is quiet cultural-religious continuity. **Never default to Rwanda/Kashmir** — these are chosen for the religion-origins framing of *this* funder.
