# Research brief — Roberto Cimetta Fund: General Fund for cultural & artistic mobility

- **Title**: General Fund for Artistic and Cultural Mobility (Euro-MENA region)
- **Funder / host**: Roberto Cimetta Fund (RCF), international non-profit registered in France
- **Channel**: partnership (mobility / relationship-building)
- **Link**: https://www.cimettafund.org/en/
- **Deadline**: **Rolling / on-demand** — the General Fund is open to all eligible travel-grant requests year-round; RCF assesses in periodic rounds. Apply well before the intended travel date (travelling before a decision is at the applicant's own risk).
- **Budget / award size**: **Travel grants** (flights + associated travel costs) for a single mobility. It funds movement, not production — a scouting/relationship budget, not edition funding.
- **Eligibility**: artists and cultural operators **living and working in Europe, North Africa and the Middle East**, with **priority to mobility from the South and East** of the region. **Italy is listed in the eligible "South of Europe" zone**, so INLAND (Italy-based) qualifies. The trip must show potential for constructive, long-term impact on the arts sector in the applicant's own country and a networking/capacity-building dimension.

## Fit score: **3 / 5**

**Rationale**: Small and travel-only, so it can never fund an edition — but it is a **clean-eligibility, low-burden foothold** for the exact upstream step INLAND's model depends on: getting a scout/editor or commissioned photographer **on the ground** in a Mediterranean or MENA territory to meet custodians and secure the local partner an edition needs. It complements the Institut français Mediterranean and Anna Lindh threads already on the board, and uniquely reaches **North Africa / the Mashriq**, which Creative Europe / Culture Moves mobility does not cover. Held at 3 by the modest scope.

## Possible territory / angle (per research-angle.md)

Use a Cimetta grant as the **first field contact** for a Mediterranean-rim edition where the INLAND angle is strong and a local partnership still needs to be built in person:

- **Northern Morocco (Rif / Tétouan–Chefchaouen)** — endangered crafts, mountain-village ritual and agrarian life; pairs with the Institut français Mediterranean route already being pursued.
- **Tunisia (islands / inland oases)** — the "Palm in the Sea" edition already drafted for Institut français; a scouting trip to name custodians and confirm seasonal scenes.
- **Southern/eastern Mediterranean generally** — priority given to South/East mobility means a return leg (a partner travelling to Italy) can also be framed.

The grant funds the *meeting*, not the magazine; frame the trip around named prospective custodians/partners and a concrete networking outcome.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)

- Mobility request form (purpose, destination, dates)
- Statement of the trip's long-term impact on the arts sector + networking dimension
- Invitation / contact at the destination (helps but confirm current requirements)
- Applicant CV / track record (published issues)
- Estimated travel budget
