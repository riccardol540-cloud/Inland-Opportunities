# Research brief — CatchLight Global Fellowship 2027

- **Funder / host:** CatchLight (San Francisco; global programme)
- **Channel:** public fund (fellowship / production grant)
- **Link:** https://www.catchlight.io/global-fellowship
- **Deadline:** ~2026-12-15 (2027 cycle; **dates to confirm** — CatchLight opens the Global Fellowship each autumn at the Night of Photojournalism in Paris, with an ~one-month application window; the 2025–2026 cycle ran 15 Nov → 16 Dec 2025).
- **Budget / award size:** US$30,000 to each Fellow, plus professional development, mentorship and support to execute a high-impact visual-storytelling project. **Free to submit** (via Submittable).
- **Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies):** Open worldwide to professional and early-to-mid-career visual storytellers — explicitly including photographers, videographers, filmmakers, multimedia creators, illustrators and educators. INLAND is a visual-storytelling publisher producing photography, digital stories and **films**, so it can apply via a commissioned visual storyteller or as a project team around an edition.
- **Fit score: 3/5.** Rationale: production-scale ($30k) support for visual storytelling that includes film and multimedia — INLAND's exact output mix — and a genuinely global, open-eligibility, free application. Held at 3 (not higher) because CatchLight foregrounds **social impact, community activation and innovative distribution / field sustainability**, which is a reframing of INLAND's slow-print anthropological model: a competitive application needs a tailored distribution/community-engagement angle for the chosen edition, not just the edition itself.

## Possible territory / angle

**Open** — pairs with an in-progress or upcoming edition where an *innovative distribution / community-activation* layer is credible. Frame the fellowship project as: an edition's documentary body of work (photography + film + digital) plus a concrete plan to circulate it back to and beyond the custodian communities — e.g. bilingual local screenings/exhibitions, an oral-history return archive, or partnership distribution with a territory's cultural institutions. Choose the territory from an already-fundable INLAND edition rather than proposing a new one here; the fellowship funds the *storytelling + distribution*, not the territory selection.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)

- Project proposal (the visual-storytelling project + its distribution / community-impact plan)
- Work samples / portfolio (photography, film or multimedia)
- Applicant bio / CV and role
- Budget / use-of-funds outline
- (No fee — submitted via Submittable)
