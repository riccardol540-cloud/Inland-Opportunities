# Research brief — The Aftermath Project: 2026 Post-Conflict Grant

- **Title:** The Aftermath Project — 2026 Post-Conflict Grant
- **Funder / host:** The Aftermath Project (US non-profit; founder Sara Terry)
- **Channel:** Public fund (photography production grant)
- **Link:** https://www.theaftermathproject.org/application/2026-post-conflict-grant-application
- **Deadline:** 2026-12-01 (midnight PST). *Not urgent (34 days out).* Free to enter.
- **Budget / award:** **US$25,000** production grant to one photographer, plus up to four finalists.
- **Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies):** Open to photographers of any nationality working on post-conflict issues anywhere in the world. INLAND applies via a commissioned photographer.

## Fit — 3 (possible, with a register caveat — surfaced for the humans)
This is surfaced the way the Carmignac row was: real budget and real territory, but a **framing tension** the collaborators should weigh before pursuing. The fund's subject is explicitly *post-conflict* — "the aftermath of conflict." Read literally that leans toward the crisis/reportage register INLAND avoids ("continuity over crisis; tension, not attack").

But there is a genuine INLAND reading: the Project's own language is about **what comes *after*** — memory, rebuilding, return — which is almost word-for-word INLAND's single question, *"what survived, and can still be witnessed today?"* The grant becomes a 5 only if the proposal tells aftermath as **lived cultural continuity** — craft, song, faith and return resuming in once-emptied places — rather than as the wounds themselves. Recommend pursuing **only** if that angle is agreed.

## Candidate territory / angle (per research-angle.md)
A post-conflict place where the aftermath is carried through culture, not spectacle:

- **Western Balkans — Bosnia's Drina/Podrinje valleys:** returnee families re-opening summer pastures (*katun* transhumance), reviving dairy craft, weddings and *sevdah* song in villages emptied in the 1990s. Named returnees, locatable hamlets, a real summer season.
- **Georgia** alternative — **Abkhaz/Ossetian displaced** communities (IDP settlements) keeping polyphonic song, the *supra* feast and highland foodways alive in exile.

The conflict stays **background**; the lens is the quiet return of ordinary life. No adversarial framing of any state.
