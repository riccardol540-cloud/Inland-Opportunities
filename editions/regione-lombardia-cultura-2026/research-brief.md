# Research brief — Regione Lombardia, cultural contributi 2026

- **Funder / host:** Regione Lombardia — Direzione/Assessorato alla Cultura (INLAND's home regional government).
- **Channel:** Public funding (regional culture fund).
- **Link:** https://www.bandi.regione.lombardia.it/ (l.r. 50/1986 "iniziative e manifestazioni di rilievo regionale"; and "Avviso Unico Cultura 2026")
- **Deadline:** **l.r. 50/86 sportello open until 16 November 2026** (LIVE — ~40 days out as of the scan). Avviso Unico Cultura 2026 runs on its own windows — confirm the current one.
- **Budget / award size:** l.r. 50/86 — **€2,500–€20,000** per initiative (fondo perduto; €990,000 pot). Avviso Unico Cultura — contributi across patrimonio culturale, spettacolo dal vivo, musei, biblioteche/archivi (amounts by ambito).
- **Fit score:** **3 / 5** — possible, home-region public money, with an eligibility + framing constraint.
- **One-line rationale:** Genuine public funding in INLAND's own region and absent from the board, but channelled to **non-profit bodies** and, for l.r. 50/86, to **events/exhibitions ("manifestazioni")** rather than a publication — best as a Lombardy-anchored exhibition/public-programme foothold (and a route to patrimonio support via the Avviso Unico), accessed through a non-profit partner.

## Why it's relevant
`sources.md` (Channel 1) names "regional culture bandi (Lombardia and others relevant to INLAND's base)". These are the clearest home-region public instruments: low-burden, recurring, and useful to seed a Lombardy edition or to stage the public-facing half of one (exhibition, screenings, talks) that a production grant can't cover.

## Eligibility (the binding constraint)
Both instruments fund **public and private non-profit bodies** (associations, foundations, committees, APS, social cooperatives, public entities). **INLAND as a for-profit publisher is not a direct beneficiary** — it participates through a Lombardy non-profit cultural partner that hosts the manifestazione / patrimonio project, with INLAND as documentation & publishing partner. l.r. 50/86 funds *events of regional significance* (festivals, exhibitions, conferences), not a magazine print run; the Avviso Unico Cultura has a *patrimonio culturale* ambito closer to documentation.

## Candidate territory / INLAND angle (within Lombardy)
Territory set by the funder's geography — **Lombardy** — found afresh per `research-angle.md`:
- **Cremona violin-making** (UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage) — named luthier custodians, living master–apprentice transmission; stage as an exhibition + publication of the workshop world.
- **Orobie / Valtellina transhumance** — *bergamini* herder families, mountain-dairy cycle, the autumn move to the plain; a seasonal, locatable, named-custodian thread.
- **Po valley / Lomellina** water-and-rice cultures (mondine memory, river fishing) — an under-documented lowland Lombardy world.
