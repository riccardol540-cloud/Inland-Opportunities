# Research brief — The Alexia Grants 2026 (Professional / Vision Grant)

- **Funder / host:** The Alexia Foundation (Newhouse School, Syracuse University, USA)
- **Channel:** public fund (photography grant)
- **Link:** https://www.alexiafoundation.org/grants (submissions via Visura)
- **Deadline:** 2027-02-13 (11:59 pm EST). The "2026" professional cycle is open now.
- **Budget / award size:** **Vision Grant US$20,000** + Sony camera/lens (runner-up US$750; awards of excellence US$250); plus three **US$5,000 "Pop-Up" grants** on set themes (conservation, environmental change, global health). **US$50 submission fee** (waivers available via partner organisations).
- **Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies):** Open internationally to professional photographers/filmmakers. INLAND applies via its commissioned edition photographer with an in-progress or proposed body of work. The Vision Grant is a real production-scale sum (US$20k), not just a token prize.
- **Fit score: 3/5.** Rationale: the Alexia brief rewards "socially significant visual storytelling that provides insight into issues around the world and **fosters cultural understanding**" — a register an INLAND cultural-continuity edition can answer honestly, at production scale. Held at 3 by the grant's stronger "inspire meaningful change" / social-issue lean (INLAND must frame continuity-and-dignity as the socially significant story, not bend toward advocacy) and the individual-photographer applicant model.

## Possible territory / angle

**Lead candidate — the pursued Albania edition's iso-polyphony custodians** (Lab/Tosk singers around Gjirokastër / Përmet / Vlorë): an endangered, UNESCO-recognised oral tradition held by named, ageing custodians in locatable villages, with real seasonal gatherings — framed as a story that *fosters cultural understanding* of a European minority soundworld before it thins out. Ties directly to a board territory already in "pursuing."

Alternatives (any carried by the edition's photographer): **Georgia** highland custodians (Issue 02 continuation); **Sardinia / Barbagia** women's *attittu* laments (also a women-carried thread for IWMF); the **Arbëreshë** endangered language and ritual.
