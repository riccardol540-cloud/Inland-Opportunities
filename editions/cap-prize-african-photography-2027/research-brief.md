# Research brief — CAP Prize (Contemporary African Photography)

- **Title**: CAP Prize — International Prize for Contemporary African Photography
- **Funder / host**: CAP Prize (awarded annually since 2012; presented in partnership with photo basel and international photography institutions)
- **Channel**: public-fund (international prize / exposure award)
- **Link**: https://www.capprize.com/rules
- **Deadline**: **Annual, ~7 February** (11:59pm Zurich time) for the cycle launched the previous year — so the next cycle closes on/around **7 February 2027**. Confirm when the 2027 call formally opens.
- **Budget / award size**: **Exposure award**, not a cash production grant — 5 winners (from 25 shortlisted) receive a **series of exhibitions** with international photography institutions and events, plus visibility with a jury of curators, publishers and editors. Value is reach and relationships, not funding.
- **Eligibility**: **open to everyone**, any age, level or descent. Work must be a **coherent series of 10–25 images created on the African continent, or engaging with an African diaspora** (dating back no more than two generations). Single images not permitted. INLAND-commissioned photography from an African edition qualifies directly; INLAND applies via / nominates its edition photographer.

## Fit score: **3 / 5**

**Rationale**: Register and eligibility are a clean match — long-form, coherent photographic series about life on the continent — and it maps directly onto INLAND's **two in-progress African editions (Rwanda, Angola)**. It is a **3, not higher**, because it is an **exposure award with no production budget**: it can't unlock an edition, but it is a low-burden, high-visibility platform that builds exactly the curator/publisher/institution relationships INLAND's funding model runs on, and puts the Rwanda/Angola body of work in front of the right eyes.

## Possible territory / angle (per research-angle.md)

Enter a **coherent 10–25 image series** drawn from an in-progress African edition, built around the quality bar (named custodian · locatable place · seasonal scene):

- **Rwanda** — a tight series on a single thread from the Goethe Africa work (e.g. the *inanga* zither master; or *urwagwa* banana-beer as social infrastructure) rather than a survey, to read as one cohesive body.
- **Angola** — a single-thread series from the drafted Lusophone edition.

Because this is the photographer's award, the strongest submission is one **photographer's** cohesive series, not a magazine sampler. Keep INLAND's continuity-over-crisis discipline: no conflict/aftermath framing.

## Required application documents (for the Producer)

- Coherent series of **10–25 images** (≥2500px long side, ≤5MB each)
- Series title + short project statement
- Photographer details / bio
- Submission fee (confirm the 2027 amount on capprize.com)
