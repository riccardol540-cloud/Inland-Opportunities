# Research brief — Creative Europe: European Cooperation Projects (CULTURE) [RADAR: 2026 closed, annual]

- **Title:** European Cooperation Projects (Creative Europe — CULTURE strand), call CREA-CULT-COOP
- **Funder / host:** European Union — Creative Europe programme (EACEA)
- **Channel:** public-fund (EU culture cooperation)
- **Link:** https://culture.ec.europa.eu/funding/calls/2026-european-cooperation-projects · call fiche: ec.europa.eu Funding & Tenders portal (CREA-CULT-2026-COOP)
- **Deadline:** **RADAR — 2026 round CLOSED 5 May 2026.** This is the EU's flagship annual culture-cooperation call; the next round typically **opens in autumn** for a **spring deadline**. The date field (~2027-05-05) is an **ESTIMATE** for the next cycle — confirm exact dates on the Funding & Tenders portal when the 2027 work programme publishes.
- **Award:** ~€60M pool, ~150 projects. **Small Scale** (min 3 entities from 3 countries; up to 80% co-funding) and **Medium Scale** (min 5 entities from 5 countries; up to 70%). Grants scale from tens of thousands into the hundreds of thousands of euros by tier.

## Eligibility (why INLAND qualifies)
Open to **all cultural and creative sectors except audiovisual-only** projects/consortia — so **publishing, photography, heritage and place-based storytelling are in scope**. **Italy is eligible.** INLAND applies as (or with) a partner in a consortium: Small Scale needs **3 organisations from 3 different eligible countries**; Medium Scale needs **5 from 5**. This is the single most on-profile EU **production-scale** route for an INLAND edition and, until now, was **absent from the board** despite being named in `sources.md`.

## Fit — 4/5
Production-scale budget, a cross-border framing that is a **natural home for INLAND's borderland / minority / cultural-continuity work**, and multi-year timelines that can actually cover field embedding and print. Held at **4 (not 5)** by the real friction: it requires a **genuine multi-country consortium** and **co-financing** (20–30%), which INLAND must assemble with partner organisations — a heavier lift than the photo-prize routes, and only actionable once the next call opens.

## Possible territory / angle
A cross-border theme that spans several eligible countries fits the consortium requirement *and* the INLAND angle. **Lead candidate: the Aromanian (Vlach) world of the southern Balkans** — a stateless, Latin-derived-language pastoral people whose transhumance, polyphonic song and craft survive across **Greece, Albania, North Macedonia and Romania** (all Creative Europe countries), anchorable to an Italian lead partner. Named custodians (a Metsovo or Voskopojë shepherd-cantor lineage), locatable highland villages, and a seasonal scene (the summer move to high pasture; autumn descent) — borderlands, minority language and endangered knowledge in one frame, naturally "European cooperation." Alternatives: an **Adriatic minority-language cooperation** (Arbëreshë ↔ Albania ↔ Arvanites) or **Alpine transhumance** across Italy–Austria–Slovenia.

## Required application documents
- Project description (cooperation objectives, European added value, cross-border dimension)
- Consortium: 3 (Small) or 5 (Medium) partner organisations from that many eligible countries + partnership agreements/letters
- Budget showing the required co-financing and work packages
- Timeline / work plan; partner track records
- Registration on the EU Funding & Tenders portal (PIC/PADOR)
